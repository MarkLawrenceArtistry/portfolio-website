WHAT ARE MINOR REVISIONS?

  • Text & Copy: Fixing typos, updating prices/dates, changing short text, or swapping font styles.
  • Color Adjustments: Changing colors of text, backgrounds, overlays, or accents.
  • Layout Tweaks: Nudging elements, aligning/centering text, or slightly resizing existing items.
  • Simple Swaps: Adjusting opacity, removing small details, or replacing a client-provided logo/photo.

WHAT ARE MAJOR REVISIONS?

  • Subject Replacements: Swapping out main characters/heroes, re-cutting renders, or rebuilding background shapes.
  • Canvas Resizing: Changing dimensions or aspect ratios (e.g., square post to vertical story) requiring layout restructuring.
  • New Additions: Adding new visual assets, heavy effects, or large blocks of text omitted from the original brief.
  • Note on Full Overhauls: Changing the entire theme, art style, or overall concept after draft approval requires a new commission order.
