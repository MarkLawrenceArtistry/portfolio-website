WHAT ARE MINOR REVISIONS?

  • Text & Copy: Fixing typos, updating prices, changing a word or sentence, or changing the font style.
  • Color Adjustments: Changing the color of text, backgrounds, or minor elements.
  • Layout Tweaks: Moving objects around, nudging elements, centering text, or scaling items (making things slightly bigger/smaller).
  • Minor Edits: Adjusting opacity/transparency, removing small elements, or swapping out a single basic image/logo provided by the client.

WHAT ARE MAJOR REVISIONS?

  • Concept Changes: Changing the core theme, art style, or overall vibe of the design after it has already been drafted.
  • Subject Replacements: Replacing core heroes, main subjects, or completely rebuilding background shapes (as noted in your graphic).
  • Layout Overhauls: Full layout changes or changing the canvas size/aspect ratio (e.g., changing a square post to a tall banner, which requires rearranging everything).
  • New Elements: Adding new custom illustrations, subjects, or large chunks of text that were not included in the original Design Brief.
